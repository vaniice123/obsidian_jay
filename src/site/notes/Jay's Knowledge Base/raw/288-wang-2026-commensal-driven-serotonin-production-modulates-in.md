We identified gut microbiota as key regulators of efficacy of IDS-based therapies and that disrupting commensal-host interactions markedly improves drug and gene delivery. Intestinal epithelial cells sense microbial stimulation and remotely activate Kupffer cells through serotonin production, thereby driving hepatic IDS clearance.
