## Abstract

Respiratory symptoms are prevalent in the general population, and they are associated with a decline in lung function and increased mortality. The gut–lung connection suggests intestinal dysbiosis may impact lung diseases, with Akkermansia muciniphila showing promise in regulating extraintestinal diseases. However, its application in patients with respiratory symptoms lacks clinical trial evidence.
