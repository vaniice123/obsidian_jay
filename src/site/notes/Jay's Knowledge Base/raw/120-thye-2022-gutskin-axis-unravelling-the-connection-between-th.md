## Abstract

Evidence has shown that gut microbiome plays a role in modulating the development of diseases beyond the gastrointestinal tract, including skin disorders such as psoriasis. The gut–skin axis refers to the bidirectional relationship between the gut microbiome and skin health. This is regulated through several mechanisms such as inﬂammatory mediators and the immune system. Dysregulation of microbiota has been seen in numerous inﬂammatory skin conditions such as atopic dermatitis, rosacea, and psoriasis.
