## Abstract

Qualiﬁed presumption of safety (QPS) was developed to provide a generic safety evaluation for biological agents to support EFSA’s Scientiﬁc Panels. The taxonomic identity, body of knowledge, safety concerns and antimicrobial resistance are assessed. Safety concerns identiﬁed for a taxonomic unit (TU) are where possible to be conﬁrmed at strain or product level, reﬂected by ‘qualiﬁcations’. No new information was found that would change the previously recommended QPS TUs and their qualiﬁcations.
