## Abstract

The pathogenesis of ulcerative colitis (UC) is unclear, but it is generally believed to be closely related to an imbalance in gut microbiota. Roseburia intestinalis (R. intestinalis) might play a key role in suppressing intestinal inﬂammation, but the mechanism of its anti-inﬂammatory effect is unknown. In this study, we investigated the role of R. intestinalis and Toll-like receptor 5 (TLR5) in relieving mouse colitis.
