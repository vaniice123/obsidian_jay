## Abstract

The beneficial effects of Akkermansia muciniphila (Akk) on gut health and inflammation reduction have been demonstrated; however, scientific evidence of hair growth enhancement by Akk has not been reported. Therefore, this study was undertaken to investigate the effect of Akk on improving testosterone-mediated hair growth inhibition. Hair growth inhibition was induced through subcutaneous injection of testosterone into the shaved dorsal skin of C57BL/6 male mice.
