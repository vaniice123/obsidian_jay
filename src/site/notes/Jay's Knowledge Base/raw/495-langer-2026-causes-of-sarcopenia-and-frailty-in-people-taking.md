## Abstract

Medications based on glucagon-like peptide 1 pathways are an efficient drug treatment for people with obesity, often eliciting rapid and pronounced weight loss. However, concerns have been raised regarding accelerated muscle wasting and sarcopenia. Here, we discuss some of the latest research in this area.
