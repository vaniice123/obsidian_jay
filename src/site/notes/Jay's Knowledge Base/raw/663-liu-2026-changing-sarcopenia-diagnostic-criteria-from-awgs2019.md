## Abstract

The Asian Working Group for Sarcopenia (AWGS) updated its diagnostic criteria in 2025, shifting focus from diagnosing sarcopenia in older adults towards promoting muscle health across the lifespan. How this update impacts the diagnosis prevalence and prediction for health outcomes in surgical patients is unknown.
