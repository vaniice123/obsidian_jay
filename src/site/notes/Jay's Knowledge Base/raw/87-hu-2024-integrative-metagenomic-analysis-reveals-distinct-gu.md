## Abstract

Obesity is a metabolic disorder closely associated with profound alterations in gut microbial composition. How‑ever, the dynamics of species composition and functional changes in the gut microbiome in obesity remain to be comprehensively investigated. In this study, we conducted a meta-analysis of metagenomic sequencing data from both obese and non-obese individuals across multiple cohorts, totaling 1351 fecal metagenomes.
