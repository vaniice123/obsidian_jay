## Abstract

Akkermansia muciniphila is a mucin-degrading bacterium of the intestinal niche, exerting beneﬁcial effects on the host metabolic proﬁle. Accumulating evidence indicated Akkermansia as a promising therapeutic probiotic against metabolic disorders such as obesity, type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular diseases. However, in speciﬁc intestinal microenvironments, its excessive enrichment may be not beneﬁcial.
