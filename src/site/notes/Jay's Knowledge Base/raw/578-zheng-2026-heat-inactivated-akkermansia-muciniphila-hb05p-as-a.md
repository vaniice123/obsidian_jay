# Heat-inactivated *Akkermansia muciniphila* HB05P as a postbiotic preparation supports muscle homeostasis under dexamethasone challenge

## Abstract

Heat-inactivated microbial preparations are attracting growing interest as stable and safe functional ingredients for food and nutrition applications. This study investigated the effects of heat-inactivated Akkermansia muciniphila HB05 (HB05P) as a postbiotic on muscle homeostasis under dexamethasone (Dex)-induced catabolic stress. In differentiated C2C12 myotubes, HB05P (4 × 104–106 cells/mL) protected against Dex-associated reductions in cell viability and myotube diameter. In Dex-induced muscle atrophic mice, oral HB05P (2 × 108–1010 cells/mouse/day) improved muscle strength, exercise capacity, lean body mass, muscle tissue mass, and myofibril cross-sectional area, while also alleviating circulatory markers associated with muscle injury. At the molecular level, HB05P suppressed the Dex-induced upregulation of the atrophy genes Atrogin-1 and muscle RING-finger protein-1 (MuRF1) while restoring the myogenic genes myogenic differentiation 1 (MyoD) and Myogenin in vitro and in vivo. HB05P also normalized the myostatin/insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1) axis and promoted phosphorylation of the protein kinase B (Akt)/mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR)-forkhead box O3 (FoxO3a) signaling network, suggesting a potential contribution of this pathway to rebalancing muscle protein anabolism and catabolism under Dex challenge. These findings identify HB05P as a postbiotic preparation with potential applications in food and nutrition biofunctionality for supporting muscle homeostasis.
