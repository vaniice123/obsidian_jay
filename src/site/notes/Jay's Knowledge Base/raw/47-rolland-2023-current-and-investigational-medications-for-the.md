## Abstract

Sarcopenia, defined as the loss of muscle mass and function, is a widely prevalent and severe condition in older adults. Since 2016, it is recognized as a disease. Strength exercise training and nutritional support are the frontline treatment of sarcopenia, with no drug currently approved for this indication. However, new therapeutic options are emerging. In this review, we evidenced that only very few trials have focused on sarcopenia/sar­ copenic patients.
