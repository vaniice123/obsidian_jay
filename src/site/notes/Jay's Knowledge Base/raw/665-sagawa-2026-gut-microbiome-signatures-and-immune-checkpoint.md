## Abstract

Gut microbial features have been associated with immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) outcomes, but whether these signatures are robust across gastrointestinal (GI) tumor types, treatment regimens, and populations remains uncertain. This critical narrative review assesses human evidence, experimental mechanisms, longitudinal sampling, and the criteria needed for clinical implementation.
