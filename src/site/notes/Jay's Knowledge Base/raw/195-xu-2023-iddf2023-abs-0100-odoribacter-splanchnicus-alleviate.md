## Abstract

Background Gut microbiota is necessary for the development of Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), and IgA-coated bacteria is correlated with susceptibility to colitis. Screening for immune-responsive taxa according to IgA coating is crucial for therapeutic target development and prognosis prediction of IBD. We found that Odoribacter. splanchnicus (O.s) can be coated by IgA and explore the relevance of O.s to colitis and the immune-related mechanism.Methods The relative abundance of IgA-coated O.s was confirmed by IgA-seq in a UC cohort before and after fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT). In vivo experiments investigated the inflammatory and barrier damage therapeutic potential of O.s against colitis. The possible mechanism of O.s was analyzed by RNA-seq.
