## Abstract

Sarcopenia, an age-related disease, leads to reduced muscle mass, strength, and function. Owing to the lack of a cure, it is crucial to determine effective prevention strategies. Akkermansia muciniphila HB05P, derived from Korean breast milk, has shown promise in enhancing muscle strength and mass in aged C57BL/6J mice. A. muciniphila HB05P treatment decreased Atrogin-1 (which is linked to muscle protein degradation), while increasing Follistatin, a protein essential for muscle growth.
