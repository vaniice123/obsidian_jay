## Abstract

IONIS-FXIRX (BAY2306001) is an antisense oligonucleotide that inhibits the synthesis of coagulation factor XI and has been investigated in healthy volunteers and end-stage renal disease (ESRD) patients. FXI-LICA (BAY2976217) shares the same RNA sequence as IONIS-FXIRX but contains a GalNAc-conjugation that facilitates asialoglycoprotein receptor (ASGPR)-mediated uptake into hepatocytes. FXI-LICA has been studied in healthy volunteers and is currently investigated in ESRD patients on hemodialysis.
