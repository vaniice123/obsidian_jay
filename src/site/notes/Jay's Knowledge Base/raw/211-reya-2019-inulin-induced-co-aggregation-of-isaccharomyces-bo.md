## Abstract

The synbiotic potential of Saccharomyces boulardii and inulin was evaluated on mice gut microflora. The number of microorganisms in the feces of mice treated with the synbiotic combination or Saccharomyces boulardii/inulin alone was found to be less compared to the normal control. S. boulardii grown in presence of inulin was found to interact and form aggregation with Escherichia coli and Enterococcus faecalis in vitro though the same interaction pattern was not seen with Staphylococcus aureus, Lactobacillus casei, Lactobacillus rhamnosus and Lactobacillus plantarum.
