## Abstract

Abstract
            We report on a comprehensive analysis of the gut microbiomes of patients with gastrointestinal (GI) cancer receiving anti–PD-1/PD-L1 treatment. The human gut microbiota has been associated with clinical responses to anti–PD-1/PD-L1 immunotherapy in melanoma, non–small cell lung cancer, and renal cell carcinoma. We aimed to investigate this association in GI cancers. We also identified bacterial taxa with patient stratification potential.
