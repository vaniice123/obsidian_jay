## Abstract

Objective. This randomized, double-blind, placebocontrolled, crossover trial evaluated the pharmacodynamic effects of a single 100-mg dose of ABT-639, a peripherally active, selective T-type Cav3.2 channel blocker, with the intradermal capsaicin pain model using pregabalin 300 mg as a positive control.
