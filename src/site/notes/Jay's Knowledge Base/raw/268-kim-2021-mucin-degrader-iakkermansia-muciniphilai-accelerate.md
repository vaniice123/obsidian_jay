## Abstract

Mucin-degrading bacteria are densely populated in the intestinal epithelium; however, their inter­ action with intestinal stem cells (ISCs) and their progeny have not been elucidated. To determine whether mucin-degrading bacteria play a role in gut homeostasis, mice were treated with Akkermansia muciniphila, a specialized species that degrades mucin. Administration of A. muciniphila for 4 weeks accelerated the proliferation of Lgr5+ ISCs and promoted the differentia­ tion of Paneth cells and goblet cells in the small intestine (SI).
