## Abstract

Background: Muscle atrophy means a progressive decrease in muscle mass, strength, and quality and has a lot of discomfort in daily life. High‑dose or continuous use of glucocorticoids (GCs), which are negative muscle regulators, led to the risk of muscle atrophy and weakness. Up to now, the effect and the underlying mechanism of Citri unshius Pericarpium (CP) on muscle atrophy have not been fully elucidated.
