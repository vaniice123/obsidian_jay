## Abstract

Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) is an incretin hormone that controls energy homeostasis, and impaired GLP-1 secretion or responsiveness is associated with metabolic diseases. Much effort has been made to develop long-lasting GLP-1-based therapeutics, whereas frequently associated with drug resistance and side effects. GLP-1 is predominantly released postprandially by enteroendocrine cells, while programmable GLP-1 delivery is rarely explored.
