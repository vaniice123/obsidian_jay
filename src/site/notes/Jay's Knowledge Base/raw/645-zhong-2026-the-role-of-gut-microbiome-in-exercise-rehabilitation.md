## Abstract

Exercise rehabilitation improves health across multiple diseases, partly through modulation of the gut microbiome. However, current understanding of the mechanisms linking exercise, microbial remodeling, and rehabilitation outcomes remains limited. This review summarizes evidence on exercise-induced microbiome adaptations across diseases and proposes the concept of “intestinal rehabilitation”, integrating patient stratification strategies, candidate biomarkers, and microbiome-targeted interventions to advance personalized rehabilitation strategies, particularly for individuals unable to perform conventional exercise.
