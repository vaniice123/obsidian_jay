## Abstract

Some human colonic Bacteroides strains carry conjugal tetracycline resistance (Tcr) elements, which are thought to be chromosomal. We have found that some of these Tcr elements can mediate the appearance of plasmidlike forms in Bacteroides uniformis 0061. When B. uniformis 0061, containing a conjugal Tcr element designated Tcr ERL, was grown in medium containing tetracycline (1 microgram/ml), two circular DNA forms were found in the alkaline plasmid preparations: NBU1 (10.3 +/- 0.5 kilobases) and NBU2 (11.5 +/- 0.5 kilobases).
