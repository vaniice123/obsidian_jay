## Abstract

A high-fat diet induces alterations in gut microbiota composition, which can be partially or completely restored through targeted dietary interventions. The present study investigated the effects of pasteurised Akkermansia muciniphila, Specialized Pro-resolving Mediators (SPMs), and B vitamins on host metabolism and gut microbiota composition in C57BL/6J mice under standard (SD) or high-fat (HFD) conditions.
